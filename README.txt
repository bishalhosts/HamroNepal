# 🇳🇵 Hamro Nepal — Calendar, Atlas & News

Hamro Nepal is a web-based platform that brings together Nepal's calendar, interactive geographical maps, district and province information, and local news resources in one place.

The platform allows users to explore all 7 provinces and 77 districts through interactive SVG maps. Visitors can click district boundaries, search for districts by name, and open dedicated district and province pages.

## ✨ Key Features

- Interactive SVG map of all 77 districts of Nepal.
- Complete map of all 7 provinces.
- Individual province and district maps.
- Clickable district boundaries with dedicated information pages.
- District search functionality.
- Province and district information.
- Nepal calendar with AD and BS date support.
- Nepal-time clock.
- District-specific news and Facebook links.
- Shared light and dark theme.
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Consistent navigation and footer across pages.

## 🗺️ Nepal Atlas

The atlas includes:

- `nepal-provinces.svg` – Map of all 7 provinces.
- `nepal-districts.svg` – Interactive map of all 77 districts.
- `provinces/` – Individual province maps with district boundaries and names.
- `districts/` – Individual SVG maps for all 77 districts.

Each map shape includes an ID and data attributes for easy styling and interaction.

## 📰 District News Management

District-specific news and Facebook links can be managed through:

`District/district-links.js`

The website automatically separates Facebook links from other news websites and displays them on the corresponding district pages.

## 🎨 Design and User Experience

- Clean and consistent interface.
- Responsive layouts across all screen sizes.
- Maximum content width of 1440px.
- Shared Nepal-time header clock.
- Light and dark mode with saved theme preference.
- Smooth calendar and map navigation animations.
- Easy navigation between districts, provinces, and maps.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5 – Website structure.
- CSS3 – Styling and responsive layouts.
- Vanilla JavaScript – Interactivity and functionality.
- SVG – Interactive geographical maps.
- Nepali Date Converter – AD and BS calendar support.

## 📍 Map Data and License

District boundaries are based on the MIT-licensed `nepal-geojson` geographical data, simplified for web display.

These maps are intended for informational and educational purposes, not surveying or legal boundary determination.

## 🎯 Project Purpose

The main goal of Hamro Nepal is to make Nepal's geographical information, calendar system, and district-level news resources easily accessible through a simple, interactive, and user-friendly platform.

**Explore Nepal. Discover Every District. Stay Connected.**
