# Hamro Nepal — Calendar, Atlas & News

**Explore Nepal. Discover Every District. Stay Connected.**

Hamro Nepal is a digital platform that brings together Nepal's calendar, interactive geographical atlas, and district-level news resources in one place.

## ✨ Features

- Nepali calendar with AD and BS dates.
- Interactive maps of all 7 provinces and 77 districts.
- Individual province and district pages.
- Clickable district boundaries and district search.
- District-specific news and Facebook links.
- Automatic Nepal-time clock.
- Light and dark theme.
- Responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## 🛠️ Technologies

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- SVG Maps
- Nepali Date Converter

## 🗺️ Atlas

Explore Nepal through interactive provincial and district maps. Select any district to view its dedicated page and geographical information.

## 🎯 Purpose

To make Nepal's geographical information, calendar system, and local news resources easily accessible through one simple and user-friendly platform.
